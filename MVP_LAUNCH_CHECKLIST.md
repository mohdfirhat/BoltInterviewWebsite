- "Interview anxiety affects 75% of job seekers"
- "Limited access to quality interview practice"
- "Traditional coaching is expensive ($100-300/hour)"
- "Remote work makes video interviews critical"

#### **2. Solution Demo (8 minutes)**
**Live Demo Flow:**
1. **User Registration** (30 seconds)
   - Quick signup process
   - Industry selection
   - Professional onboarding

2. **Interview Setup** (1 minute)
   - Choose industry (Technology)
   - Select difficulty level
   - Voice AI interviewer introduction

3. **Live Interview Experience** (4 minutes)
   - AI asks realistic question with professional voice
   - User responds naturally
   - Smooth question progression
   - Real-time feedback indicators

4. **Results & Feedback** (2 minutes)
   - Detailed performance analysis
   - Speaking metrics (pace, clarity, confidence)
   - Personalized improvement suggestions
   - Progress tracking dashboard

5. **Business Model** (30 seconds)
   - Free tier for user acquisition
   - $29/month Pro subscription
   - Clear upgrade path and value

- "Growing remote work trend"
- "AI technology advantage"
- "Scalable global opportunity"
